10102C / Override

Opening pickups and first scoring routes

Take the easy value, then leave a useful return route.

Working draft · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Related maneuver: Recover space while scoring.

Agreed direction

Upright yellow stacks of cup and pin are convenient pickups, especially with a claw and no intake. Take them when the pickup and placement are quick. Do not spend the opening chasing a fallen or awkward stack while an easier scoring cycle is open. After cleanup, reds can go farther out while we still have time to return toward Left.

The return route is part of the cycle

Scoring farther away is acceptable while there is time and space to come back. If an opponent flips Left and leaves, regain the area as part of the next useful scoring route. Flip when a good opportunity appears. Do not turn a temporary toggle loss into an unproductive chase.

New proposal · needs practice

Re-evaluate after a missed pickup or blocked approach instead of repeating it automatically. The opening plan should name a second useful goal and a return path, not demand the same first goal every time.
