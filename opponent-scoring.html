10102C / Override

Responding to opponent scoring

Separate auto result from the ongoing scoring matchup.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Two independent questions

Did we win auto? And are we now outscoring them on the routes actually available? An auto win can coexist with a losing driver-control matchup. An auto loss can coexist with a winning one.

AutoDriver matchupInitial bias
WonWinningKeep reliable cycles; do not buy unnecessary toggle exposure.
Lost / tiedWinningKeep the productive race; use a limited yellow burst if the finish budget needs it.
WonLosingUse the bonus as time to transition, not proof that we can camp.
Lost / tiedLosingPrepare controllable yellow value or a realistic ownership swing earlier.

Matchup details that matter next

Opponent pairingLikely issueOur proposed adaptation
Two strong scorersBoth can score, then split late attacks.Track both approach windows; require a stronger finish cushion.
Two competent mid/high robotsSustained scoring with credible defense.Use observed cycle advantage, not reputation, to choose the branch.
One exceptional scorer + weak partnerTheir weak robot can camp cheaply.Do not assume occupying that bot wins the trade; create space against the scorer.
Strong defender + limited scorerContested lanes may dominate raw speed.Preserve separate routes and favor stable color value.
New proposal · needs practice

Estimate the matchup from repeated clean opportunities rather than one dropped pin. Record why a cycle failed: robot execution, blocked route, supply, or stack height. The fix depends on the cause.
