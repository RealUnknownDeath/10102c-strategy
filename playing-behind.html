Playing from behind

Match the response to the size and cause of the deficit.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Agreed direction

SituationResponseExit condition
Slightly behind, cycles competitiveOne robot adds a few yellows, or both briefly do so; maintain red scoring.Return to sustainable cycles when the projected gap is covered.
Lost auto and slightly behindRecalculate the finish with the auto deficit; a “small” field gap can be much bigger overall.Do not stop the recovery burst just because raw stacks look similar.
Consistently outscoredBuild N2, establish Down coverage, and use the stronger scorer to grow southern value.Hold only when the finish margin beats remaining counterplay.
Massively behindOur normal low-risk hold cannot create enough value. Seek a reachable ownership swing or productive open area.Pick a concrete score-changing objective instead of splitting into two idle defenders.

The expansion has a price

Red-1’s trip to Down loses scoring time. The stronger scorer’s return to Left may be long. If blue reaches Left as we begin stacking N4, both valuable areas may need attention at once. Do not expand merely because we are losing: expand because the new points and controllable ownership exceed the transition cost.

New proposal · needs practice

Delay extra N4 yellow investment until Down coverage is established. If the transition is blocked, keep using R2 for useful color scoring and preserve a retreat; do not continue a half-completed plan as if Down were secure.
