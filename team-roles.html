10102C / Override

Team roles and robot capabilities

Give the faster scorer the job that keeps producing points.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Agreed direction

10102C starts as the bottom robot and is normally the stronger scorer. Red-1 starts at the top. Early on, each works its own goal rather than both queueing at the same stack. Once we expand to Down, the weaker scorer usually becomes the Down defender. The stronger scorer retains Left responsibility while continuing to score on the southern goals. “Left responsibility” does not mean sitting still at Left.

When to swap

Swap when current positions make it quicker, a robot cannot score the needed stack height, or one driver is better at the defensive task. Make the handover explicit: the incoming robot confirms coverage before the outgoing robot leaves. Do not send both robots across the same lane to make the names match the diagram.

New proposal · needs practice

Assign three things before the match: primary scoring area, first retreat point, and who watches the second opponent. Keep the scoring/defending assignment provisional until the first few clean cycles reveal the actual matchup.

What remains unmeasured

Travel and scoring times, lift reach, cup orientation reliability, recovery after a miss, pushing control, and whether a defender can score locally without opening a toggle approach. These stay unknown until the robot is ready.
