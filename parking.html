10102C / Override

Parking and partner coordination

Parking is part of the score comparison, not an automatic final action.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Agreed direction

In the two-toggle recovery branch, we are prepared to concede both blue parks and not park ourselves. Left’s defender may be able to leave if the area is genuinely safe; Down’s defender is more exposed and usually has less freedom. Use the score and opponent positions to decide.

The marginal robot can matter more than eight points

A robot entering MID can also change its yellow ownership. With four visible yellow halves in MID, changing a 0-red/1-blue finish to 1-red/1-blue denies blue 40 yellow points and adds our 8 parking points: a 48-point margin improvement, before the cost of abandoning a toggle. If blue has two in MID, our first robot earns 8 but does not change the majority.

Compare complete finishes

Compare “both defend,” “one defends and one parks,” and “both park” using the ownership each choice leaves behind. Do not add parking points while silently keeping the departing robot’s toggle defense. The calculator derives MID ownership directly from the parking counts.

New proposal · needs practice

If we can leave safely, first ask whether one robot changes the majority or only adds its own eight. That distinguishes a high-value move from a small bonus that risks a much larger quadrant.
