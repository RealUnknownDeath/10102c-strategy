10102C / Override

5. Choose the finish

Recheck the safe margin and both opponents before leaving a defended area.

Agreed safety rule · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

  • Departure creates any risk or the return is uncertain → Stay, defend and score locally only with coverage
  • Parking is safe for the defended zone → Evaluate the park and MID finish
  • Another toggle is open and departure is safe → Evaluate useful yellow value and the return route
  • We need to test the contact-neutralized defensive finish → Use the conservative finish calculation
  • The projected lead is still insufficient → Continue recovery rather than camping too early

A full capture by blue is a separate defensive-failure scenario. Contact neutralization, no red parks and two blue parks are inputs for the conservative stay-and-defend test, not assumptions for every ending.
