10102C / Override

Alternative match strategies

Options to practice, not automatic replacements for our main plan.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Our current main plan

Score continuously, use reds for a stable base, concentrate early yellows near Left, then expand to Down when the scoring race demands it and coverage is credible. Preserve the ability to shorten routes and defend at the finish.

Three useful alternatives

ProposalWhen to try itFailure to watch
More color, less exposureWe already win clean cycles and can cover the projected finish without heavy yellows.Too slow against a yellow-heavy opponent that retains ownership.
One concentrated yellow areaWe can defend one approach well but cannot cover Left and Down together.One successful late touch erases a large share of our score.
Pre-endgame MID investmentA realistic majority finish is available and perimeter control is poor.Parking tie neutralizes MID yellows; do not place in the final ten seconds.
New proposal · needs practice

Use the minimum additional yellow exposure that covers the projected gap. Once a recovery burst succeeds, resume reliable color cycles instead of continuing to inflate the same vulnerable stack.

Do not confuse variety with strength

Different plans should have different triggers and tradeoffs. For practice bots, variety should come from matchup, auto outcome and position—not random abandonment of the assigned strategy.
