Playing with a lead

Keep scoring until the lead survives their remaining options.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

A visible lead is not yet a secure lead

Our yellow points may disappear or change sides. Before camping, evaluate the likely finish after giving blue the MID and parking outcome we are prepared to concede. Then allow for what they can still score or steal. The secure-lead document walks through this with a shared diagram and calculation.

Continue productive cyclesTake reliable points while maintaining a recoverable defensive position.
Shorten the routeAs the attack window closes, favor nearby work and preserve both responses.
Hold only after the testStop cycling when every credible remaining opponent branch still leaves us ahead.

What we should watch

Watch Blue 2, not only the robot at our toggle. A defender can feint to draw 10102C away while the free scorer takes the other approach. Count the points tied to each quadrant and compare that exposure with our non-yellow margin.

New proposal · needs practice

Maintain two spoken estimates: “ahead if our yellows hold” and “ahead if our yellows disappear.” A team can be winning the first comparison while losing the second. That distinction tells us whether defense is optional or essential.

A positive calculator margin is conditional on its finish state and future-swing allowance. It does not prove that our robots can enforce those assumptions.
