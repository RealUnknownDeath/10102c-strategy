10102C / Override

Responding to opponent defense

Their defender is a problem to route around, not automatically a robot we must chase.

Working draft · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

One opponent camps Left

Stop adding yellow value that blue can retain at Left. We still have R2, N4 and, before the placement cutoff, MID as possible scoring destinations. Choose among those based on Blue 2’s location. An open goal is useful only if we can approach, place and leave.

One opponent follows our scorer

The discussed response is to move 10102C to a useful northern route. If blue follows, Red-1 may gain southern scoring freedom. If blue does not follow, 10102C gets a productive lane. This works only if the alternative is actually scoreable and the journey does not cost more than it creates.

They leave Left to attack Down

The proposed retreat is Red-1 backing toward Down from the lower-loader side while the attacker travels around R1/R2. 10102C then recovers Left. The shorter-looking path is not proof of a successful defense: starting speed, facing, clearance and Blue 2’s interception matter.

New proposal · needs practice

Against a camper, compare one full alternate scoring cycle with one realistic recovery attempt. Prefer the action with better net match value, including the other robot’s scoring during that time.

What happens next?

  • Productive route available → Use it
  • Still consistently outscored → Reassess recovery
  • Camper leaves → Recover space and control
