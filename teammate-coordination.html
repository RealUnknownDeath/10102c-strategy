10102C / Override

Teammate coordination and role switches

One scorer, one defender is a temporary assignment—not a permanent robot identity.

Working draft · October 5, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Agreed direction

For a modest deficit, one bot can add yellows while the other keeps scoring reds, or both can make a brief yellow burst. If we are consistently being outscored, Red-1 builds N2, optionally scores some reds on N1, then moves to Down. The stronger scorer keeps Left responsibility while actively adding value to southern goals. The Down holder scores only when it can return safely.

The handover must leave one answer to each threat

Before Red-1 leaves N2, decide who reacts if Blue 1 goes to Left. Before 10102C crosses to N4, decide who reacts if Blue 2 cuts the return route. “Both retreat” is incomplete unless the retreat paths and responsibilities still fit together.

New proposal · needs practice

When the two jobs collide, keep the robot already covering a threatened area there. Route the free robot to the next scoring job. Swap identities only when it improves travel or execution, not to preserve a fixed diagram.

Future bot teams should share these assignments. A bot paired with a human should infer and support the human’s chosen lane, with simple optional instructions. See practice and game handoff.
