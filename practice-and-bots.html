Future game changes

The simulator needs selectable auto win/tie/loss starts with consistent stacks and bonus, shared team-level plans, assigned goal/loader responsibilities, and transitions driven by score and position. A pair of bots should coordinate closely. A human’s bot partner should support the human’s route and accept optional simple directions such as cover a toggle, score here, or follow. No named play-call system is required.

Bot variants can emphasize color scoring, concentrated yellow control, late toggle pressure, or MID. Each needs coherent triggers and recovery behavior. Difficulty should change execution and judgment, not grant hidden score or knowledge unavailable to the practice setup.

What a benchmark should include

Test mirror matches and each counterstrategy under all auto outcomes, both strong/strong and strong/weak pairings, multiple starting arrangements, and repeated runs. Track strategy adherence separately from navigation and mechanism failures. One winning result is not evidence that a strategy is generally superior.
