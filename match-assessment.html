4. Read the scoring race

Revisit this decision whenever routes, scoring pace or ownership change.

Decision point · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Carry the auto result into this assessment. Compare actual useful cycles, not stack appearance or one missed pickup.

  • Our cycles are competitive and the finish is on track → Continue useful color and controlled yellow scoring
  • We have a lead → Check whether it survives remaining counterplay
  • A modest deficit needs extra value → Choose a limited yellow response
  • We are consistently being outscored → Evaluate the Down transition and role handover
  • They camp or obstruct our routes → Find a productive route and account for Blue 2
  • They pressure the defender while scoring elsewhere → Define the response without wasting every cycle
