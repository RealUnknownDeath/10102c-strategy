Opening after an auto loss

Use the result without pretending we have already chosen the auto routine.

Working draft · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Starting decision

Expect more urgency for yellow value, but first determine whether we lost only the bonus or also lost the scoring race and field position. A small recoverable deficit does not require sending both robots into permanent defense.

Read the fieldCheck upright pickups, useful stacks and current access.
Read the matchupCan our two robots score faster on their available routes?
Choose the responseKeep scoring, add a short yellow burst, or prepare the two-toggle transition.
