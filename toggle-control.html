10102C / Override

Toggle control and productive defense

Defend the points at risk without abandoning useful scoring.

Working draft · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Related maneuver: Recover space while scoring.

Protect a safe margin of victory

Agreed October 6: We always aim for a safe winning margin. If leaving creates any risk to the toggle zone we are defending or to that margin, we stay in the zone. We can keep scoring locally until the end, provided the scoring action preserves coverage.

Camping both toggles is a conditional finish, not our default. When departure is safe, we may park or take an open toggle with useful yellow value. Leave only when they cannot take the area we vacate before time expires, or we can reliably return or intercept them in time. If that is uncertain, stay.

Parking generally preserves a shorter return route in our intended setup. A last-second trip to another perimeter toggle may not. Check the actual positions and route before either move; extra points never justify risking the defended zone.

Agreed direction

The scoring route can control space. When they do not attack while we score farther away, we can spend the closing portion working nearby goals and respond from there. The discussed “last 30 seconds” is a planning estimate, not a measured universal cutoff. If they take Left early and leave, we can reclaim space first and flip later.

What camping actually costs

An enemy stationed at Left loses some scoring freedom, but so can our defender. The trade is attractive only when our remaining scorer and our retained points beat their remaining scorer and their endgame. A weaker blue robot may be a very cheap camper while their strong robot continues scoring.

Neutralizing is not stealing

If a quadrant contains six visible yellow halves, taking it from red to neutral removes 60 red points. Taking it from red to blue removes those 60 and adds 60 to blue: a 120-point margin swing. A short late neutralization can therefore matter even if they cannot camp all match.

New proposal · needs practice

Defend against arrival time, not merely whether an opponent looks nearby. If we cannot finish a cycle and return before they can reach the toggle, shorten the cycle or hand over coverage. Measure that comparison once the robot is ready.

What happens next?

  • Only an approach, not a committed attack → Examine the response boundary
  • Coverage restored → Return to the scoring assessment
  • Final approach → Choose the finish
