10102C / Override

Pressure without taking the toggle

Open branch: the opponent interrupts local scoring while its partner keeps producing points.

Open question · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Related maneuver: Shorten the cycle and retreat.

This is an exploit to test, not a proven counter. Their weaker robot repeatedly approaches Down and backs off; their stronger robot scores elsewhere. If every approach makes our defender stop prematurely, they may gain in the scoring race without capturing a toggle.

The decision still to define

Where must Blue 1 reach before Red-1 abandons the local scoring action and commits to defense? Draw that boundary and the return route. Account for facing, current action and Blue 2; do not invent a fixed distance before practice.

Two outcomes to distinguish

If continuing the action would put coverage at risk, defend. If the approach is still answerable after a safe local action, scoring may remain possible. The unresolved part is identifying that boundary reliably, not relaxing the rule against a risky departure.

What happens next?

  • They commit to the approach → Preserve coverage
  • They back off → Reassess productive scoring
  • Time is running out → Choose a safe finish
