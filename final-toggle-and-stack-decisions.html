10102C / Override

Final toggle and stack decisions

Choose the action that improves the final margin, including what it sacrifices.

Working draft · October 6, 2026 · Agreed direction plus clearly marked proposals

Related maneuver: Remove an own-goal liability.

Protect a safe margin of victory

Agreed October 6: We always aim for a safe winning margin. If leaving creates any risk to the toggle zone we are defending or to that margin, we stay in the zone. We can keep scoring locally until the end, provided the scoring action preserves coverage.

Camping both toggles is a conditional finish, not our default. When departure is safe, we may park or take an open toggle with useful yellow value. Leave only when they cannot take the area we vacate before time expires, or we can reliably return or intercept them in time. If that is uncertain, stay.

Parking generally preserves a shorter return route in our intended setup. A last-second trip to another perimeter toggle may not. Check the actual positions and route before either move; extra points never justify risking the defended zone.

First distinguish the outcomes

Left red retains its yellow value. Left neutral gives that yellow value to neither side. Left blue gives it to blue. The best reachable result may be neutralization rather than a full recovery. Our own defender must also release the toggle before it can count as set.

R1/R2 knockdown fallback

If blue will keep a toggle, removing a yellow-heavy stack from our own alliance goal can deny points. But check every visible colored half that falls with it. Removing our red halves loses our points too. This is an emergency comparison, not an automatic last-five-seconds action.

Do not extend this fallback to N2, N4 or another neutral goal. The planned N2 stack remains an ownership obligation.

A simple comparison

Suppose a removable R1 stack would give blue 40 yellow points but also gives us 10 red points. Removing the whole stack improves our margin by 30, before considering travel and other lost opportunities. If Left will instead be red, removing the same stack destroys 50 of our points. Re-evaluate after the toggle changes.

New proposal · needs practice

Assign one robot to any removal attempt and one to preserve the rest of the finish. Never assume a removal is selective, instant or guaranteed until tested with the actual stack.

What happens next?

  • Positions or threats change → Recheck the finish
  • Need to count the outcome → Evaluate margin and uncertainty
